Remorse Chess - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Remorse Chess is a free chess game by Shareef Faddah, based on a chess variant created by Bardelo Bardalini. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on Android, Windows or in a web browser.

Information we collect

None. Remorse Chess does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Data saved on your device

To remember your game, the app saves your game settings, the current game in progress and whether you have seen the intro screen. This is stored only on your own device, using the local storage built into your browser or app. It is never sent anywhere. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet access

The game runs entirely on your device and does not need an internet connection to play. The app does not send any data about you or your games to us or to anyone else.

Third parties

Remorse Chess does not use third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services. The app is distributed through Google Play and the Microsoft Store, which have their own privacy practices that apply to your use of those stores.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
